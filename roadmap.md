### ✨ What's New

- **🍿 Unified Media Requests (Movies, TV Shows, Ebooks & Audiobooks):** A single 1-click request hub to search, discover, and request movies, TV series, ebooks, and audiobooks with real-time status tracking and automated library delivery.
- **🤖 Autonomous AI Support Assistant:** Your in-app conversational helper equipped with live playback diagnostics, stream health probes, missing media searches, automated replacement grabs, and 1-click support ticket escalation right from your stream card.
- **📚 Ebooks & Audiobooks Subsystem:**
  - **In-Browser Reader:** Built-in reading experience with Bookerly typography, customizable margins, color themes, and persistent reading progress.
  - **1-Click Send-to-Kindle:** Dispatch any book directly to your Amazon Kindle devices and apps in seconds with zero manual configuration.
  - **Floating Audiobook Player:** Interactive HTML5 audio player featuring chapter selection, disk track ordering, continuous background autoplay, and playback speed controls.
- **⚡ Live Streams Doctor & Transcode Diagnostics:** Real-time playback telemetry, in-browser server speed test, and tailored device fix checklists via Transcode Doctor to resolve buffering and unlock 1080p/4K Direct Play.
- **🎁 New Personalized Invite Links:** Easily generate personal invite links to invite family and friends to DomsHomeLab with seamless account onboarding.
- **🛡️ New Account Types (Kids & Living Room):** Dedicated sub-profiles with customizable age ratings (Kids / YA / Adult), child-safe library filtering, and secure PIN protection tailored for shared family living room TVs.
- **🗂️ Custom Library Selection:** You can now pick and customize exactly which Plex media libraries you want accessible and visible on your account.
- **⚡ Radarr & Sonarr Super User Accounts:** Direct, secure access to advanced movie and TV management tools for authorized power users to monitor downloads, inspect releases, and manage queue priorities.
